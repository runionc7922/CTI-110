# Sandwich Construction

## Assumptions
- We have an unobstructed counter top
- On the counter we have:
    - a plate with two slices of bread side-by-side
    - jar of PB
    - jar of jelly
    - a butter knife
    - napkin
    - the robot is charged and ready to go
<br><br><br>

## Steps to build the sandwich
1. Open the PB
2. Open the Jelly
3. Pick up the knife
4. Use the knife to scoop PB from jar
5. Spread PB on left slice of bread
6. Use napkin to clean knife
7. Use the knife to scoop Jelly from jar
8. Spread Jelly on right slice of bread
9. Use napkin to clean knife
10. Discard napkin
11. Lay down knife
12. Stack bread with PB & Jelly sides touching
13. Replace lids on PB & Jelly
14. Put PB, Jelly, and knife away

<br><br><br>

## Exit Conditions
- Plate and constructed sandwich are only items on counter
